Our Story

Creating cozy design templates to bring warmth, intentionality, and high aesthetics back to your digital feeds.

Behind SuminCreates

SuminCreates started out as a personal collection of templates. In a digital environment increasingly dominated by rigid, generic layouts and overly busy promotions, we wanted to build design spaces that invite deep breaths, relaxation, and simple joys.

Inspired by vintage scrapbooks, paper cut-outs, clean typography, and warm, cozy color schemes, we craft assets designed specifically for digital storytellers, writers, lifestyle brands, and boutique creators. We combine editorial precision with a soft, warm vibe so that your content stands out by inviting connection, not just attention.

Our Design Philosophy

Intentional Whitespace: We believe that what you leave out is just as important as what you put in. Ample margin and blank spaces let your photos breathe and capture the eye organically.

Warm Color Accents: Our templates focus on cozy, warm palettes—terracotta, soft peach, dusty roses, and rich creams—creating a visual landscape that feels like a quiet coffee shop corner.

Effortless Customization: Every design is optimized specifically for Canva. This means you do not need expensive software, graphic design degrees, or coding experience to look like a premium publisher. You can easily drag and drop your photography and customize colors to match your unique brand in under 5 minutes.

Join Our Community

Beyond our paid collections, we publish free Canva templates, design tutorials, and creative guides every week to support self-starting creators. We are so happy to have you as part of our journey as we build quiet, beautiful places on the internet together.
