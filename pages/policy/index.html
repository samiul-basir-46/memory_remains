Return Policy

Custom products are non-refundable. Since all our products are custom-made specifically for you using your photos, we cannot accept returns or exchanges once production has started.

When we accept a return / replacement

  • Product arrived damaged or broken during delivery
  • Wrong item delivered (different product than ordered)
  • Significant print quality issue (blurry, color mismatch) due to our error

We cannot accept returns for

  • Change of mind after placing order
  • Low quality photos submitted by customer causing blurry prints
  • Incorrect delivery address provided by customer
  • Product already used or opened

How to raise a return request

  1. 1 Contact us within 48 hours of delivery via WhatsApp at +880 1622-000471
  2. 2 Send clear photos/video of the damage or issue
  3. 3 Share your order ID and the name used to place the order
  4. 4 Our team will review and respond within 1–2 business days

For faster resolution, you can also use the Request Return form.
